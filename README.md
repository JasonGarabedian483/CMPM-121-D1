## Game Introduction

To play the game you must click on the target to increase your money. The target will move around requiring you to keep moving to click it. Upgrades increase the amount of money you gain passively or how much money the clicking gives.

Tooling adapted from the [CMPM 121 Fall 2026 S01 starter](https://github.com/ucsc-cmpm-121-f26/s01-starter/tree/8974888efb6537be82e8eb069db139ea885039da), which has no explicit repository license.
